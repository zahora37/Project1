Bread born from patience & practice

Every loaf starts the same way it has for five years — a spoonful of living starter, carefully fed and nurtured through the week, then mixed by hand on Friday evening.

The dough cold-ferments overnight in our home fridge, developing the complex, tangy flavor and open crumb you can't rush. Saturday morning, the oven comes on at 6 AM.

We limit our bakes intentionally. A small number of loaves means every one gets full attention — from first mix to final score.
