Quick answers

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions? +

All our breads contain gluten. Our kitchen also handles dairy, eggs, tree nuts, and seeds. We cannot guarantee allergen-free products, so please consult your doctor if you have a severe allergy.

How do I store my sourdough? +

Keep it cut-side down on a board for 2–3 days at room temp, or wrap in beeswax wrap. For longer storage, slice and freeze — toast straight from frozen.

Can I cancel or change my order? +

Yes! Email us or reply to your confirmation email by Thursday at 6 PM. After that, we've already begun preparing your dough and can't make changes.
